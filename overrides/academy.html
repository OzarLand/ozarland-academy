Part of OzarLand

OzarLand: a shared land for better OpenStack operations. It started as a personal initiative by Soufian Zaouam to share practical OpenStack operational knowledge, and it is meant to grow into a space where operators exchange experience, document solutions and help each other run more reliable platforms. It is open to everyone: your knowledge, questions and corrections are welcome.
