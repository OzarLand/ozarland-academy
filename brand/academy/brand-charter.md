# OzarLand Academy — brand charter

OzarLand Academy brings nine open learning modules together under the OzarLand promise: **A shared land for better OpenStack operations.** The Academy is one island trail: a prelude, stop 0 (Begin, LUMO's village), then eight named stops—Discover, Deploy, Operate, Intervene, Go to production, Compare, Upgrade and Equip. The separate book is the same island's quiet archive for deeper reading.

**Voice.** Serious, honest and kind. Write for beginners and experts without talking down to either. Explain what is known, what the source proves, what remains uncertain, and what a safe next step looks like.

**Visual world.** Keep the established hand-painted island, bright sky, turquoise sea, white stone, terracotta roofs, timber, parchment, brass, green vines and navy cloud pennants. Lumo remains the same curly auburn-haired cartographer with head goggles, cream tunic, navy cloud cape, satchel, compass and brass lantern. Lumo accompanies the reader as a patient guide; never make the character childish, authoritative or alarmed.

**Color.** Parchment-soft is the light canvas; deep navy carries reading text, links and primary controls. The dark canvas is blue-black with parchment text. Lantern gold marks keyboard focus and active exploration. Module accents distinguish places; separate semantic risk colors distinguish read-only, state-changing and destructive operations. Accent colors are not small body text.

**Type and layout.** Use system fonts only. Keep prose at 1.6 line-height, code in a system monospace stack, and content within a comfortable reading measure. Use generous spacing, restrained 8–20 px radii, one-pixel borders and very light shadows. Keep command output, tables and source explanations on solid surfaces.

**Images and icons.** Painted images support orientation, empty states and page headers; they do not sit behind technical prose. Flat 48 px icons and stage seals stay simple, consistent and labelled in adjacent text. All illustration text remains outside the image.

**Accessibility.** Underline body links, include visible focus, check contrast, preserve text labels with color, support dark mode and reduced motion, and keep mobile reading free of page-level horizontal scroll. Alt text states only what is visibly present; decorative images use empty alt text.
