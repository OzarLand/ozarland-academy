# Character references of the learning path

Reference sheets for the art direction of stops 1 to 7. They are not published on the site: they keep LUMO and the
recurring characters the same from one generated image to the next. Attach them to the image generator with the
prompts of the art direction.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `lumo-evolution.webp` | LUMO from stop 0 to stop 7, from left to right. LUMO changes from an apprentice builder into a cartographer, one accessory per stop, never a new costume: 0 work apron and tools, 1 a nearly blank map and a small compass, 2 route pins, 3 a reading book, 4 a field notebook, 5 the full-size brass lantern, 6 a brass key on the belt, 7 a full satchel with a nearly complete map. |
| `lumo-expressions.webp` | The six expressions: attentive curiosity, concentration, honest doubt, caution, understanding, shared pride. |
| `lumo-turnaround.webp` | LUMO from the back, the front and the side (stop 7 outfit). |
| `cast.webp` | The recurring characters of the learning path, with LUMO first and Noor (stop 0, in a wheelchair) on the last row. |

What never changes: curly auburn hair, goggles and patterned headband, the same face and proportions, the cream shirt,
the navy cape with a plain white cloud and a brass compass brooch, the leather satchel, brown trousers and laced boots.
