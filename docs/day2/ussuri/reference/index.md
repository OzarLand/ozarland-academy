# Reference

The reference material of the study: the headline findings, the glossary, the generated tables, the per-role fact
sheets, the Ansible listings, and the method and limits of the study.

<img src="../assets/images/S3-concepts-and-reference.webp" width="1536" height="1024" loading="lazy" alt="Ilyan and LUMO stand between a table of building models and brass columns and a bookcase of plain boxes, books and scrolls.">

## Pages

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Technical findings](findings.md)** — Fifteen headline findings (F1–F15), each with its evidence and why it matters.
- **[Glossary](glossary.md)** — The terms the study uses, with the place where each is defined.
- **[Generated inventory](generated-inventory.md)** — Tables generated by `tools/extract_kolla_inventory.py`: the plays
  executed by `site.yml` in order, the containers defined by each role, the role task structure, handlers and
  helper-role usage, and the templates that reference inventory groups.
- **[Per-role fact sheets](factsheets/index.md)** — Five fact sheets produced by systematic source reading of
  `ussuri-eol`, how they were checked, and the eleven corrections that apply to them.
- **[Ansible listings](listings/index.md)** — Ansible 2.9.27's own parse of the unmodified Kolla-Ansible `ussuri-eol`
  playbooks: the `--list-tags` listing of `site.yml` and its `--list-tasks -t nova` listing. Nothing was executed
  against a host.
- **[Method and limits of the study](method-and-limits.md)** — The six phases of the study, and its limits: source code
  only and no timings.

</div>
