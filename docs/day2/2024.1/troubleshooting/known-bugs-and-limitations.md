# Known bugs and limitations

The implementation limitations and bugs found in Kolla-Ansible `2024.1-eom`, by area, with the evidence and the status
of each row. The rows carried over from the 2023.1 study keep their area and their order; where an issue no longer
holds in 2024.1, the row says so, with the code that shows it. Rows that are new in 2024.1 are marked so and come after
the row of their area. Where a Status cell adds [NOT AVAILABLE], it names what the sources do not establish about the
issue.

> [!NOTE]
> **Key answers**
>
> - **Implementation limitations and bugs of `2024.1-eom`, by area:** CLI, `site.yml`, `kolla_container` (the module
>   that replaced `kolla_docker`), the kolla images and individual roles (table below).
> - **Still present in 2024.1:** the `kolla_serial` override, the `--ask-vault-pass` and `--include-dev` oddities, the
>   cyborg `serial` play, the placement loadbalancer include, the `--check` blind spots
>   ([F14](../reference/findings.md#f14)), the HAProxy restart instead of reload and its stale files, the RabbitMQ
>   cookie, the MariaDB, Keystone, Glance, Cinder, Heat and Swift items, the masakari `qemu+tcp` URI, the Docker restart
>   of `bootstrap-servers` and the certificates that are never renewed.
> - **No longer holding in 2024.1:** the outward RabbitMQ `pull` bug (outward RabbitMQ is removed) and the ovs-dpdk
>   handler name mismatch (the notify now matches). Still not holding, as in 2023.1: the ignored stop and client
>   timeouts ([F3](../reference/findings.md#f3)), the `-t common` no-op ([F2](../reference/findings.md#f2)), the
>   neutron-dynamic-routing upgrade gap, the haproxy upgrade task, the octavia items, the masakari SASL mismatch and
>   `docker_custom_option`.
> - **New in 2024.1:** the Podman worker treats check exit codes differently from Docker; the first 2024.1 check of a
>   container whose unit an earlier release wrote reports drift ([F16](../reference/findings.md#f16)); the cyborg
>   endpoints use two variables that nothing defines; Octavia fails `prechecks` with the defaults unless Redis is
>   enabled; stale single-external-frontend files; and, newly recorded, the `genconfig`/HAProxy interaction.
