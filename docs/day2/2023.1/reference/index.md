# Reference

The reference material of the study of Kolla-Ansible `2023.1-eol`: the headline findings, the glossary, the generated
tables, the per-role fact sheets, the Ansible listings, and the method and limits of the study.

<img src="../assets/images/S3-concepts-and-reference.webp" width="1536" height="1024" loading="lazy" alt="Ilyan and LUMO stand between a table of building models and brass columns and a bookcase of plain boxes, books and scrolls.">

## Pages

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Technical findings](findings.md)** — Fifteen headline findings (F1–F15), each with its evidence, its 2023.1 status
  and why it matters. F2, F3 and
  F11 no longer hold in 2023.1; F14 changed in part.
- **[Glossary](glossary.md)** — The terms the study uses, with the place where each is defined.
- **[Generated inventory](generated-inventory.md)** — Tables generated by `tools/extract_kolla_inventory.py`: the plays
  executed by `site.yml` in order, the containers defined by each role, the role task structure, handlers and
  helper-role usage, and the templates that reference inventory groups, generated from the 2023.1 tree.
- **[Per-role fact sheets](factsheets/index.md)** — Five fact sheets first produced by systematic source reading of
  `ussuri-eol` and re-studied against `2023.1-eol`, how they were checked, and the eleven corrections with their 2023.1
  status.
- **[Ansible listings](listings/index.md)** — ansible-core 2.14.18's own parse of the unmodified Kolla-Ansible
  `2023.1-eol` playbooks: the `--list-tags` listing of `site.yml` and its `--list-tasks -t nova` listing. Nothing was
  executed against a host.
- **[Method and limits of the study](method-and-limits.md)** — The sources and versions, how the 2023.1 pages were
  re-studied
  from the Ussuri study, and the limits: source code only, no timings, no runtime or lab evidence.

</div>

## Fact sheets

| Sheet | Scope |
|---|---|
| [Fact sheet A](factsheets/a-infra-lb-ovn.md) | `loadbalancer` (haproxy, keepalived and proxysql; the Ussuri `haproxy` role), the 44 per-service `loadbalancer.yml` includes, `loadbalancer-config`, `haproxy-config`, `proxysql-config`, `memcached`, `chrony` (no role in 2023.1), `ovn-controller` and `ovn-db` (the Ussuri `ovn` role), `ovs-dpdk`, `service-cert-copy`, `certificates.yml` |
| [Fact sheet B](factsheets/b-mariadb-rabbitmq-keystone.md) | Galera bootstrap/recovery/backup and rolling restart; RabbitMQ (incl. outward); Keystone fernet keys and runtime coupling |
| [Fact sheet C](factsheets/c-glance-placement-cinder-horizon-heat.md) | Glance, Placement, Cinder, Horizon, Heat: per-role flow, bootstrap on reconfigure, dependencies, restart impact |
| [Fact sheet D](factsheets/d-barbican-octavia-swift-manila-compute-roles.md) | Barbican, Octavia, Swift, Manila: control-plane roles and every role that places containers on compute hosts; templates embedding host lists |
| [Fact sheet E](factsheets/e-host-bootstrap-ops-playbooks.md) | `bootstrap-servers` (in 2023.1 the ansible-collection-kolla roles: Docker restart conditions, `/etc/hosts`), prechecks, destroy, prune-images, fact gathering, CLI, tuning docs |
