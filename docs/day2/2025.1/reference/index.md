# Reference

The reference material of the study of Kolla-Ansible `2025.1-eom`: the headline findings, the glossary, the generated
tables, the per-role fact sheets, the Ansible listings, and the method and limits of the study.

<img src="../assets/images/S3-concepts-and-reference.webp" width="1536" height="1024" loading="lazy" alt="Ilyan and LUMO stand between a table of building models and brass columns and a bookcase of plain boxes, books and scrolls.">

## Pages

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Technical findings](findings.md)** — Twenty-two headline findings (F1–F22), each with its evidence, its 2025.1
  status and why it matters. F1 (loop-notify) no longer holds in 2025.1; F18 (a service tag no longer restarts
  HAProxy), F19 (`reconfigure` and `upgrade` cannot be batched), F20 (every compute lists all hypervisors), F21 (a
  dropped `config.json` entry restarts its container on every run) and F22 (`ovs_wrapper` does not stop Open vSwitch
  cleanly) are new in 2025.1.
- **[Glossary](glossary.md)** — The terms the study uses, read for 2025.1, with the place where each is defined.
- **[Generated inventory](generated-inventory.md)** — Tables generated by `tools/extract_kolla_inventory.py` from the
  2025.1 tree: the 71 plays executed by `site.yml` in order, the containers defined by each role (161 container
  definitions), the role task structure, handlers and helper-role usage, and the templates that reference inventory
  groups.
- **[Per-role fact sheets](factsheets/index.md)** — Five fact sheets first produced by systematic source reading of
  `ussuri-eol`, re-studied against `2023.1-eol`, `2024.1-eom` and then `2025.1-eom`, how they were checked, and the eleven
  corrections with their 2025.1 status.
- **[Ansible listings](listings/index.md)** — ansible-core 2.18.20's own parse of the unmodified Kolla-Ansible
  `2025.1-eom` playbooks: the `--list-tags` listing of `site.yml` and its `--list-tasks -t nova` listing. Nothing was
  executed against a host. They replace the ansible-core 2.16.20 listings of the 2024.1 study.
- **[Method and limits of the study](method-and-limits.md)** — The sources and versions, how the 2025.1 pages were
  re-studied from the 2024.1 study, and the limits: source code only, no timings, no runtime or lab evidence, Podman
  paths read in the code only.

</div>

## Fact sheets

| Sheet | Scope |
|---|---|
| [Fact sheet A](factsheets/a-infra-lb-ovn.md) | `loadbalancer` (haproxy, keepalived, proxysql, `haproxy-ssh`; the Ussuri `haproxy` role), the per-service `loadbalancer.yml` includes, `loadbalancer-config`, `haproxy-config`, `proxysql-config`, `letsencrypt`, `memcached`, `chrony` (no role in 2025.1), `ovn-controller` and `ovn-db` (the Ussuri `ovn` role; Southbound relays new in 2025.1), `ovs-dpdk`, `service-cert-copy`, `certificates.yml` |
| [Fact sheet B](factsheets/b-mariadb-rabbitmq-keystone.md) | Galera bootstrap/recovery/backup and rolling restart; RabbitMQ (quorum queues only in 2025.1; RabbitMQ 4.0 by default); Keystone fernet keys and runtime coupling |
| [Fact sheet C](factsheets/c-glance-placement-cinder-horizon-heat.md) | Glance, Placement, Cinder, Horizon, Heat: per-role flow, bootstrap on reconfigure, dependencies, restart impact |
| [Fact sheet D](factsheets/d-barbican-octavia-swift-manila-compute-roles.md) | Barbican, Octavia, Swift (removed in 2025.1), Manila: control-plane roles and every role that places containers on compute hosts; templates embedding host lists |
| [Fact sheet E](factsheets/e-host-bootstrap-ops-playbooks.md) | `bootstrap-servers` (the ansible-collection-kolla roles `baremetal`, `docker` or `podman`, `etc_hosts` and others: Docker restart conditions, `/etc/hosts`), prechecks, destroy, prune-images, fact gathering, CLI, tuning docs |
